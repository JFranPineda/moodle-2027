## Brand & Style

The design system projects academic prestige, high-performance rigor, and institutional luxury, fused with the sharp vitality of cutting-edge educational technology. It serves a multi-tier learning ecosystem ranging from foundational secondary education to competitive admissions and university-level mastery.

The visual language balances two forces: an authoritative, nocturnal baseline of deep blacks, obsidian containers, and refined gilded metallic accents, juxtaposed against high-energy neon color signatures for each pedagogical division. This delivers an interface that feels elite, focused, and immersive—eschewing playful, childlike ed-tech clichés in favor of discipline, ambition, and intellectual triumph.

The execution adopts a refined **Dark Minimalist Neo-Glass** aesthetic:
- **Foundational Surfaces:** Deep obsidian and carbon backgrounds (#0B0B0E, #16161D) eliminate ambient visual noise and reduce cognitive fatigue during long study sessions.
- **Institutional Aurum:** Luminous gold (#D4AF37, #FFDF73) is reserved strictly for core institutional milestones, master certifications, global navigation badges, and pinnacle achievements.
- **Neon Division Wayfinding:** Electric hues define division pathways—Escolar (Phosphor Green #00FF66), Preuniversitario (Cyan Neon #00E5FF), and Universitario (Crimson Neon #FF2A4D)—infusing energy into module cards, active tabs, and telemetry without compromising typographic clarity.
- **Precision Glass:** Subtle 1px translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with micro-diffused ambient glows replace heavy drop shadows, reinforcing an architectural, digital-campus atmosphere.

## Colors

The system is engineered as an intentional dark-mode-first environment. Colors follow a strict functional split: the institutional tier anchors governance and prestige, while division accents demarcate educational tracks and live metrics.

### Institutional Palette
- **Institutional Gold (Primary):** `#D4AF37` (Base Gold), `#FFDF73` (Highlight Gold / Hover), `#997A15` (Deep Gold Border / Active). Applied to global brand elements, premium status indicators, milestone badges, and root institutional calls to action.
- **Deep Obsidian (Neutral Surface Core):** `#0B0B0E` (Canvas background), `#121217` (Surface Base), `#16161D` (Elevated Card / Container), `#22222B` (Interactive / Hover states).
- **Pure Architectural White:** `#FFFFFF` (Primary typography, high-priority icons).
- **Muted Steel:** `#94A3B8` (Secondary reading text, meta tags), `#475569` (Tertiary lines, disabled UI elements).

### Division Palettes (Domain-Specific Contexts)
1. **División Escolar (Neon Phosphor):**
   - Accent Primary: `#00FF66` | Secondary Accent: `#10B981` | Glow Tint: `rgba(0, 255, 102, 0.15)`
   - Used for primary school metrics, foundational coursework, streak indicators, and completion chips.
2. **División Preuniversitario (Cyan Electric):**
   - Accent Primary: `#00E5FF` | Secondary Accent: `#0077FE` | Glow Tint: `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.15)`
   - Used for mock test timers, admission probability rings, STEM drill modules, and live class feeds.
3. **División Universitario (Crimson Neon):**
   - Accent Primary: `#FF2A4D` | Secondary Accent: `#DC2626` | Glow Tint: `rgba(255, 42, 77, 0.15)`
   - Used for specialized research units, thesis tracking, semester grade ledgers, and competitive rankings.

### Application Rules
- Never use full neon fills on large background areas; neon is exclusively for interactive strokes, icons, badges, indicators, and selective gradient text headers.
- Container borders use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` by default, transitioning to the respective division accent or gold on hover and active states.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Space Grotesk** for structured, forward-thinking headers and statistical callouts with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and extended learning materials.

- **Space Grotesk** introduces technical rigor, authoritative punch, and high geometric contrast. It is deployed across all headlines, hero metrics, test scores, tab navigations, and division stamps.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides humanist warmth and high legibility at micro and reading sizes, ensuring that dense academic syllabus descriptions, lecture notes, and examination questions remain legible under low-light dark mode conditions.
- **Micro-labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`)** are set with deliberate positive letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) and uppercase rendering to demarcate division tags, status pills, and institutional ranks cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 8pt geometric grid system, built to maintain institutional rigor across density-heavy academic dashboards and editorial course catalog screens.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid. Margin: `3rem` (48px), Gutter: `2rem` (32px). Maximum container constraint: `80rem` (1280px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid. Margin: `2rem` (32px), Gutter: `1.5rem` (24px). Sidebars collapse into an overlay navigation drawer.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4-column fluid grid. Margin: `1rem` (16px), Gutter: `1rem` (16px). Cards span 4 columns; auxiliary metric widgets re-arrange into horizontally swipeable carousels.

### Layout Philosophy
- Vertical rhythm follows strict multiples of `0.5rem` (8px). 
- Dashboard layouts adhere to split-view structures: a fixed 260px administrative rail, an institutional command header (56px high), and modular learning cards with internal paddings of `1.5rem` (desktop) and `1rem` (mobile).
- Dense learning views (e.g., examination simulators) adopt compact internal gutters (`0.75rem`) to maximize question visibility without requiring pagination scrolls.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque dropshadows. Instead, depth is achieved through an architectural stack of translucent tonal surfaces, micro-borders, and targeted neon-chromatic backlights.

### Surface Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#0B0B0E`. The deepest structural plane.
- **Level 1 (Structural Rail & Grouped Sections):** `#121217` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Course Cards, Exam Modules):** `#16161D` with 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover state introduces a division-colored glow: `0px 4px 20px rgba([division-accent], 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Popovers, Active Drawers):** `rgba(22, 22, 29, 0.85)` treated with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a top rim highlight: `linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02) 100%)`.
- **Level 4 (Toasts & Urgent Exam Alerts):** Deep obsidian with an illuminated 1px keyline of pure `#D4AF37` (for institutional notifications) or `#FF2A4D` (for critical timed countdowns).

### Ambient Glow Rules
Neon chromatic blooms must remain low-opacity (`0.08` to `0.18`) with large blur radii (16px to 32px). They appear solely behind primary actionable cards, ongoing live lectures, or high-tier achievements to prevent visual saturation.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a precise, architectural silhouette with restrained corner softeners. 

- **Level 1 (Soft):** Base UI components—such as buttons, input controls, table cells, and badges—use a calibrated radius of `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Modules (`rounded-lg`):** Standard modular containers adopt `0.5rem` (8px), reinforcing a structured, collegiate discipline that separates the brand from playful, over-rounded consumer applications.
- **Modals & Dialogs (`rounded-xl`):** Primary system overlays and high-order panels use `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Pill Exception:** Pure circular or pill treatment is exclusively permitted for numerical progress counters, status live badges (`rounded-full`), and avatar framing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional:** Gradient fill `linear-gradient(135deg, #FFDF73 0%, #D4AF37 100%)`, text `#0B0B0E`, font Space Grotesk Bold. Subtle 0.25rem radius. On hover: glow `0px 0px 16px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.
- **Division Action Buttons:** Base background `#16161D`, 1px solid stroke of the respective neon accent (`#00FF66`, `#00E5FF`, or `#FF2A4D`). Text matches the neon hue. Hover transitions background to `rgba([division-accent], 0.1)` with full stroke luminescence.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent fill, text `#94A3B8`, hover text `#FFFFFF` with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.

### Cards & Module Containers
- **Default Card:** Surface `#16161D`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- **Division Program Card:** Header card features an integrated 2px top accent line corresponding to the division (Green, Cyan, or Red). Hover elevates the card with a matching 12px ambient drop glow and border color brightening to `rgba([division-accent], 0.4)`.

### Chips & Badges
- Set with `Space Grotesk`, `label-sm` (10px), uppercase, letter spacing `0.08em`.
- **Style:** Translucent surface `rgba([division-color], 0.1)`, 1px border `rgba([division-color], 0.3)`, text in full neon color. Includes a 6px glowing status dot for live classes or completed state.

### Input Fields
- **Container:** Dark base `#121217`, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, corner radius `0.25rem`. Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans 14px in `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder: `#475569`.
- **Focus State:** 1px border transition to `#D4AF37` (for account/institutional forms) or the active division hue (for classroom inputs), backed by a faint `0 0 0 3px rgba([accent], 0.15)` focus ring.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Base:** 16px × 16px frame, border 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, background `#0B0B0E`.
- **Checked State:** Checkbox fills with active division color or Gold `#D4AF37`; icon rendered in `#0B0B0E`. Radios adopt an interior 6px concentric pip.

### Division-Specific Specialized Components
- **Academic Progress Rings:** SVG-based circular meters with background stroke `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` and active meter rendered in division neon with a blurred clone underlay creating a dynamic glow.
- **Timer/Exam Countdown:** Space Grotesk tabular digits set against a darkened slate glass pill with a pulsing 1px neon-red (`#FF2A4D`) perimeter alert during the final 5 minutes.
- **Grade Transcript Table:** Compact `#16161D` alternating rows, segmented with hairline dividers `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`, leading with white typography and ending with division-tinted achievement badges.
